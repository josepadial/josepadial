## 👋 About Me

I’m a **Cloud Software Architect** with a **PhD in Artificial Intelligence** and over a decade of experience building **secure, scalable, and observable** platforms for the financial sector.  

- **Builder mindset** → From architecture to production-ready solutions.
- **People focus** → Mentoring teams and enabling developer productivity.
- **Innovation with governance** → AI, CI/CD at scale, platform automation.

## ⚡ What I do

- **Cloud & Platform Engineering** – Secure, scalable, **Kubernetes**-based platforms with progressive delivery, cost governance, and **SRE** practices.
- **CI/CD at scale** – Designing and operating pipelines and delivery systems for regulated environments, including **Jenkins-as-a-Service** for banks (multi-tenant, hardened, policy-driven).
- **AI in Production** – Pragmatic ML/LLM enablement: pipelines, inference services, observability, safety/guardrails, and model lifecycle.
- **Developer Experience** – Creating reusable templates, automations, and guardrails to reduce friction and increase velocity.
